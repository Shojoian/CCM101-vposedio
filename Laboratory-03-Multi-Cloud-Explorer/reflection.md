# Mission Reflection

**1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?**
Amazon Web Services (AWS) impressed me the most due to its sheer scale and historical market dominance. The incredible variety of services it offers demonstrates why it remains the industry standard.

**2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?**
All three operate on a flexible pay-as-you-go pricing model and rely on vast global networks segmented into Regions and Availability Zones. They all provide identical foundational infrastructure pillars: Compute, Storage, Networking, and Identity Management.

**3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?**
Technical decisions must be heavily driven by specific business context. An organization reliant on legacy Microsoft software will migrate to Azure to minimize friction, whereas a company building machine learning pipelines will choose GCP for its specialized data tools.

**4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?**
I learned that choosing a cloud provider is never a one-size-fits-all technical decision. A cloud engineer must thoroughly evaluate the client's current ecosystem, budget constraints, and future scalability needs before recommending a platform.

**5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission?**
My portfolio has expanded from basic infrastructure commands to professional, comparative cloud architecture analysis. The structured Markdown documentation demonstrates an ability to write technical reports and make context-aligned architectural recommendations.
